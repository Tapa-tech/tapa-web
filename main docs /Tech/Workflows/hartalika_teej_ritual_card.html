Vidhi — step by step
Evening of 13 Sep (Dwitiya) — setup
1
Bathe and dress in green or red
2
Make sand / clay idols of Shiva, Parvati, Ganesha
3
Take the Vrat Sankalp facing east
4
Abhishek with water + milk · offer belpatra and flowers
5
Offer sindoor, bangles, shringar to Parvati
6
Listen to or recite the Vrat Katha
Night of 14 Sep (Tritiya) — jagaran
7
Stay awake through the night — bhajans, songs, reading
Morning of 15 Sep (Chaturthi) — completion
8
Morning aarti · immerse idols in water · break fast
Mantra
ॐ नमः शिवाय
Om Namah Shivaya
Chant 108 times with a mala, or 11 / 21 / 51 times
Fasting
Nirjala (no food or water, 24 hrs) is the dominant form.
Sajal (with water) accepted for health reasons.
Phalahar permitted for pregnancy, elderly, health conditions.
